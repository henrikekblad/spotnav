SpotNav 3.2.0 brings pool, spa and sauna, one notification setting per phone and the home's priority list into the app. Install `spotnav-3.2.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. It works with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.x and every 3.0 beta; what a Home Assistant version does not offer stays hidden.

## New

- **Pool, Spa and Sauna on the heat page**, as in the card: the water or room temperature and the target, what SpotNav does or would do and why, today's filtering and heating hours, **Extra filtering**, **Bathe now** / **Heat now**, the next bath or session, and the choices when one will not be ready in time. Setup and the control switches stay in Home Assistant. Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b5.
- **Not ready in time, across the home.** When cars, a bath or a sauna session compete for the same room, the plan box shows one sheet with every choice, and the app's own notice offers the three that free the most.
- **Notifications per phone.** One **Notifications** box for this phone, the same setting the card shows: whether it gets anything, which events and which chargers it follows. A phone with both the SpotNav app and Home Assistant's app is one entry and is told only once. The pool season notice can close the season from the notification. Needs 3.0.0b5; with an older Home Assistant the app's notification settings work as before.
- **The priority list** is shown read-only in the settings (it is set in the card); with an older Home Assistant the solar and charger priority settings stay as before.
- **The plan and the flow** draw the pool, the spa and the sauna as their own loads, and the heat pie counts them under Heat.
- **The car's level:** the app words the new notice when a car's level cannot be read, and a charge that goes on after a missed departure.
