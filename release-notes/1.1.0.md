SpotNav 1.1 knows which car is plugged in when several cars share a charger, and gets new, compact settings screens. It follows vehicle identification in SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.13, which it needs for the new parts. Install `spotnav-1.1.0.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## Which car is plugged in?

- When Home Assistant cannot tell which car was plugged in, the app asks: a SpotNav notification *Which car is plugged in?* with a button per car, the likeliest first (with more than three cars, the two likeliest and one that opens the app). A button answers at once, also with the app closed, and the notification then quietly says which car was selected. When the question is answered elsewhere or decided, the app takes it off. The event is on by default where Home Assistant identifies cars, and comes at once with instant notifications, otherwise at the next 15-minute check.
- The charger card shows the same question as a banner with one button per car. The first answer wins, from the app, the Home Assistant card or any phone; with no answer the car already chosen stays.
- The charger card now carries the car: its level, its charge target and, on a line of its own, how it was decided (by the car's charging cable, by position, by the camera, selected manually, or assumed). **⇄ Change car** changes the car at any time while a car is plugged in, and counts as an answer.
- Each car has its own charge target, the same at every charger, and the target follows the car when it changes.
- The status line says *Identifying the car…* while Home Assistant looks at the cars, and *Waiting for an answer: which car is plugged in?* while the question is open.

## Camera

- Where Home Assistant identifies cars with a camera, **Settings → Charger** shows the camera and its AI task as chosen in the Home Assistant card. **Crop parking spot** fetches a picture and lets you drag a selection around the parking spot, with a live preview of what is compared.
- Each car has a **Reference picture** with a **Day** and a **Night** slot: with the car parked at the charger, take, retake or delete each picture in place. A car without a reference picture is never recognised by the camera.

## Settings

- Every setting is now a value row, like the main screen's, under headings that name the charger, the site and the car. Tap a value to change it in its own small editor, which saves that value alone.
- Several paired chargers are tabs of one charger card, the main screen's charger first, and several cars are tabs of one car card.
- **Cars at this charger** and **Identification** (Automatic, Always ask, Off) are in the charger's settings, as in the Home Assistant card.
- A car's charge limit can be changed in its settings where Home Assistant can set it, and a battery size the car reports is shown read-only.
- Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.13 for vehicle identification and the camera; with an older Home Assistant the app works as before.
