SpotNav 1.0.1 is a small update to 1.0.0. Install `spotnav-1.0.1.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- The vehicle card in Settings now shows the charge level as a number: `59 %`, `~59 %` when it is an estimate, or "No reading". The sensor name is no longer listed there.
- A charger renamed in Home Assistant is followed by the app on the next read.
- The chart and the widget show the new day right after midnight, including when the app was left open overnight or the phone was asleep, instead of keeping yesterday's day for several minutes.
- The release build uses optimised resource shrinking.
- A paired charger can now leave on a chosen date, up to a week ahead. Under the departure time in the plan settings, "Every day" or "On a date" picks it; the plan summary shows "today", "tomorrow" or the weekday and date. The plan may wait for hours that are usually cheaper, and the departure is always kept. A date that has gone by is shown with a note and cleared on the next save. The unpaired local planner keeps its daily departure.
- The app now works with the Home Assistant integration 1.1: it asks for the departure date, words the new status messages (waiting for cheaper hours, held by the charger, charger disabled, waiting for the planned start, charging started outside the plan) in every app language, and ignores fields a newer integration adds instead of refusing the dashboard.
- The bundled price-area list is refreshed to the 20 areas now published. Portugal is published on the Madrid clock, and its chart changes day at 23:00 on a Lisbon clock.
