SpotNav 1.0.1 is a small update to 1.0.0. Install `spotnav-1.0.1.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- The vehicle card in Settings now shows the charge level as a number: `59 %`, `~59 %` when it is an estimate, or "No reading". The sensor name is no longer listed there.
- A charger renamed in Home Assistant is followed by the app on the next read.
- The chart and the widget show the new day right after midnight, including when the app was left open overnight or the phone was asleep, instead of keeping yesterday's day for several minutes.
- The release build uses optimised resource shrinking.
