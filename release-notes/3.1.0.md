SpotNav 3.1.0 brings the home's plan into the app. Install `spotnav-3.1.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. It works with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.x and every 3.0 beta; what a Home Assistant version does not offer stays hidden.

## New

- **The Plan box on the house page.** Everything SpotNav plans for the home on one rolling time axis, up to 36 hours: what the house takes from the grid each hour split by load, the sun and the battery, the net from the grid, the ceiling that applies (import target, power level, power tariff or main fuse) and a price strip. Under the chart **Per load** (a lane and a line of why per load) and **In words** (the plan as a list), both folded until you tap their heading. A load SpotNav only watches is drawn faded and says *would*. Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b3 or later.
- **When a charge does not fit under the ceiling.** Where an import target or a power level holds the hour and the car cannot be ready by its departure, the charger page and the car's lane in the Plan box say so, with three choices for this departure only: **Take what fits**, **Later departure** (the earliest hour by which it all fits; moves this one departure) and **Allow the peak** (asked twice). Needs 3.0.0b3 or later.
- **Heating zones, the water heater and the power level**, as in the card: each zone Controlled or Watched with its line, the water heater with its opt-in, line and tank sensor, what SpotNav holds and why, and the power level's line. Needs a 3.0 beta (b2 or later).
- The heat page's strip covers a rolling 24 hours, and the heat settings use the card's current words (*Hot water boost* for the heat pump's input, kept apart from the *Water heater* switch).
