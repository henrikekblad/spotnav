SpotNav 2.3.1 is a small fix release. Install `spotnav-2.3.1.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## Fixed

- **The chart shows the paired charger's price area.** When the screen opened, the prices were loaded for the app's own area before Home Assistant had answered, and were not loaded again when the answer named another area. The plan, its times and its cost were right, but the chart and the line under it ("Prices for …") could show another area.

## Improved

- **The help for the ENTSO-E key names the page as it reads today:** My Account → Web API Access, and Generate Token. The menu entry only appears once ENTSO-E has answered your e-mail.
