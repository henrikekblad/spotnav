SpotNav 1.0.7 follows the manual pause in SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.11: a person's Start or Stop now pauses automatic charging until the car is unplugged. Install `spotnav-1.0.7.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- The status line says what a person's Start or Stop holds and what ends it, in the Home Assistant card's own words: "Stopped manually – until the car is unplugged.", "Charging manually – until the car is full or unplugged.", "Stopped manually – until the next plug-in ends." (a Stop given with no car plugged in), and, on a charger that cannot report a plug-in, "… until you resume automatic charging." Resume automatic charging ends the pause at once, as before.
- Start and Stop say what they do on a long press (and to a screen reader): Start pauses automatic charging until the car is full or unplugged, Stop until the car is unplugged.
- A Start with no car plugged in says so ("No car is plugged in, so there is nothing to start.") instead of a Home Assistant error.
- The status line names a charger that keeps charging after it was stopped (SpotNav then sends no more stops: stop it at the charger or unplug the car).
- A settings change made right after a Start or Stop is no longer turned away with "The settings changed elsewhere": the record the command left is read at once, and a change that met only a newer revision is sent again by itself.
- When the departure comes too soon, the status line says how far the charge gets ("Will not be ready by the departure: about 44 % (7.4 kWh) by 12:00."), and a plan that could not be made says exactly why.
- Notifications from the SpotNav app itself also say when a charger keeps charging after it was stopped.
- The charge history's colours are explained as cheap and expensive.
- Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.11. With an older integration the manual pause is not shown this way.
