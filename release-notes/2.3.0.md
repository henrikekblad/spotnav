SpotNav 2.3.0 catches up with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4.1 to 2.5.0. Install `spotnav-2.3.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. Paired with an older Home Assistant integration, the new parts simply stay hidden.

## New when paired with Home Assistant 2.4.1 to 2.5

- **A warning when the grid meter's sign looks reversed.** When Home Assistant sees the grid move the other way each time the charger's draw changes, **Get started** says so in the card's words: *The grid meter's sign looks reversed … Solar and load balancing read export as import.* (and the same for a site's signed phase currents). Its button names the setting to change, such as *Turn off "The sensor shows export as positive"*, and opens the site's settings in Home Assistant, where the setting is. The row goes away by itself once the sign is right.
- **A Stop that only you can end.** At a charger that cannot say when a car is unplugged while it is stopped, and with no plug sensor on the car, the status adds *Stopped by hand. Press Start or Resume to charge again.* It is a status line only, never a notification.
- **Nord Pool from HACS.** When the HACS integration Nord Pool stands in for Home Assistant's built-in one, the electricity price says why there are no prices: keep it and enter a free ENTSO-E key in Home Assistant, or switch to the built-in Nord Pool.

## Improved

- **The hint about the charger's own current says exactly where to set it**: the site's settings in Home Assistant, Configure → Wiring for *your charger* → Measured current source, as the card says it. It used to send you to "Site wiring".
- **Terms of service** are linked at the bottom of Settings, beside Report an issue.
- **Solar priority is now called Car or home battery first, and it moved to the site's settings beside the home battery.** Its help says what it decides: who gets the sun's surplus, who goes first at the main fuse while the battery charges from the grid, and what the import target counts as import. Nothing changes in how it works.
