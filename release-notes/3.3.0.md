SpotNav 3.3.0 brings the year with SpotNav, the new Now box and one set of colours into the app. Install `spotnav-3.3.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. It works with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.x and every 3.0 beta; what a Home Assistant version does not offer stays hidden.

## New

- **The year with SpotNav.** From 10 December to 31 January a band at the top of the house and charger pages offers a short story of your year, as in the card: what SpotNav saved, the car's charge in the cheapest hours, the sun, the battery and the most expensive hour, with a picture to share or save. Once opened or closed the band does not come back on this phone; the story stays under Settings → Support until 31 January. Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 3.0.0b8.
- **The new Now box**, as in the card: the house in the middle with its heat, pool and other use around it, sun, grid, battery and car at the corners, lines that grow with the power, figures that always add up, and ≈ on what is estimated. The moving arrows stand still when the phone's animations are off.
- **One set of colours** for the same energy everywhere, as in the card, in light and dark: the car magenta, what is sold green, what is bought blue.
- **No export at negative prices** is shown in the battery settings (read only: an administrator changes it in Home Assistant).
- **Card words from Home Assistant.** The app shows the card's own words, fetched from your Home Assistant, before its own copies.
- **The house and heat details** open as a full screen, like Settings.

## Fixes

- A charge at risk of not being ready is told only while the car is plugged in, in the app's own check and in the home's not-ready notice.
- The details screen's scroll stays inside its frame.
