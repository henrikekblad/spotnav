SpotNav 1.2 gives each car a minimum charge level, follows Home Assistant's automatic charge periods, sets the car's charge levels with sliders, and speaks German, Dutch, Spanish and French. It follows SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14, which it needs for the new parts. Install `spotnav-1.2.0.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## Minimum charge level

- Each car has a **Minimum charge level** in **Settings → Vehicle**: **Off**, or 10 to 80 % in steps of 5. Like the charge target, it is the car's own, the same at every charger.
- While the car is below it, Home Assistant charges at once at the set current, whatever the strategy. The status line says *Charging to the minimum level (30 %)*. At the floor the strategy takes over; a pause or a Stop you chose always wins.
- It needs the car's charge level, and says so beside the value when the car has no level sensor.

## Charge periods

- How the charge is split into periods is now a charger setting, **Settings → Charger → Charge periods**: **Automatic** (the default: the cheapest hours, split only where the saving is worth another start, each period at least 30 minutes) or **1 period** to **8 periods**. It is no longer in the planning card.
- Without Home Assistant, the app's own planner still lets you choose up to eight charging periods.

## Sliders for the car's levels

- **Charge target**, **Minimum charge level** and **Charge limit** in **Settings → Vehicle** each open a slider, with the value shown large above it. The minimum level's slider starts at **Off** and stops at the charge target, the track beyond it hatched.
- The **Charge limit** slider covers the range and step the car's integration takes (50 to 100 % in steps of 10 on a Kia, for example), also in the charging screen's charge limit popover. Nothing is written unless you move the slider, and at most once a minute per car.

## Planning

- The planning card is titled with the car Home Assistant plans for: **Planning for EV6**.
- **Charge by** is one row showing **Energy · kWh** or **Target SoC · %**; tap the value to switch.
- With a target, the car's level, its charge limit and how old the reading is share one line. The target slider marks the level now and the car's charge limit (only when it is below 100 %), and shades the track up to the car's minimum charge level, with **min 30 %** under it. A target that is already met, or one at the car's own limit, is said once instead of an energy of 0.0 kWh.

## Status and camera

- On Solar with no car plugged in, the status line says *Solar · surplus available (4.2 kW)* while the sun would start a charge, and *Solar · no car plugged in* otherwise, in place of waiting for sun.
- A car's day and night reference pictures are shown in its settings as two equal tiles, cropped as they are compared. Tap either to open the reference dialog.
- The *Which car is plugged in?* notification, with a button per car that answers even with the app closed, works as in 1.1.

## Languages and prices

- The app, its widget and its store listing are now also in German, Dutch, Spanish and French, nine languages in all, with the same words as the Home Assistant card. The language follows the phone, or is chosen under **Settings → General**.
- The **Resolution** setting is gone: every chart, table and widget shows the prices as each area publishes them, every 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

## Needs

- SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14 for the minimum charge level, the charge periods setting, the charge limit range and the new status lines. With an older Home Assistant the app works as before, without them.
