SpotNav 1.5.0 follows SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.21 and 1.22: the electricity price shared by several chargers, phase switching for solar, a clearer wait for a weekday's prices, and fees shown as Home Assistant prices them. Install `spotnav-1.5.0.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## Electricity price

- **One price for every charger.** Home Assistant now keeps the price area, VAT, energy tax and grid fee on an **Electricity price** device that a site and its chargers share. **Settings → Electricity price** names it, for example *Electricity price SE4 · used by all 2 chargers*, and says that a change there applies to every charger using it. Area and fees are changed as before.
- **Price from.** With more than one electricity price in Home Assistant, a **Price from** row chooses which one the charger uses; on a site the choice applies to every charger on it.
- **Two prices for the same area.** After upgrading Home Assistant, when two electricity prices for one area have different fees, the card asks once which to use, with each price's VAT, energy tax and grid fee on its button, or to keep both.
- **Fees as Home Assistant prices them.** An energy tax or grid fee that is on without a figure of its own showed *Not set*; it now shows the area's suggested figure, which is what Home Assistant charges with.

## Solar

- **Phase switching for solar.** On a charger that can switch phases (Peblar for now), the charger card has **Phase switching for solar**, Off or Automatic. When the sun covers one phase but not three, the charge goes on on one phase instead of drawing the rest from the grid.
- **One phase in the status.** A solar charge on one phase reads, for example, *Solar · 1 phase · 2.9 kW*.

## Plan

- **Waiting for a weekday's prices.** The wait based on price history now says it waits for that day's prices and when they come, for example *Waiting for Friday's prices (at 13:45), which have been 44 % cheaper over the last 4 weeks*, so it no longer reads as waiting until that day.

## Wording

- **Load balancing.** The site's row is called **Load balancing**, as in Home Assistant.

Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.22.0 for the electricity price rows and phase switching, and 1.21.0 for the one-phase status; the rest works with any SpotNav for Home Assistant.
