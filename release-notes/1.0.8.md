SpotNav 1.0.8 adds "Fill" to the kWh slider, keeps the status up to date with the app open, and waits for each price area's own publication time. Install `spotnav-1.0.8.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- When Home Assistant knows the car's level and battery size, the kWh slider reaches past what the battery has room for, marks "full" on the track at that amount, and says under it how much fills the battery (and the car's own charge limit when it is below 100 %).
- The slider's last step is "Fill": the charge then follows the car and fills it to its limit at every plug-in, instead of a fixed amount. Moving the slider off the last step chooses an amount again.
- The status line says when the car is being filled, and when an amount is charged instead because the car's level or battery size is unknown.
- Each price area's own publication time: Great Britain (Agile, about 16:00 UK time) and Spain's regulated PVPC (about 20:15 Madrid time) are waited for at their own time, and a waiting line whose time has passed names no time.
- After Start or Stop, the charger card keeps its buttons in view, disabled, saying "Starting…" or "Stopping…" while Home Assistant waits for the charger, and reads the status every five seconds (for at most 45 seconds) so the buttons return as soon as the charger has answered.
- When Home Assistant cannot be reached, the app says so once, in plain words, without a revision number or a connection error on the charger card.
- The widget is drawn at its real size, so its chart and text are no longer squeezed sideways on some launchers.
- Sliders show their fill as thick as the track on newer Android versions.
- Paired charging (Fill and its status lines) needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.12. With an older integration the slider is as before.
