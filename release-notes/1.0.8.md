SpotNav 1.0.8 adds "Fill" to the kWh slider. Install `spotnav-1.0.8.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- When Home Assistant knows the car's level and battery size, the kWh slider reaches past what the battery has room for, marks "full" on the track at that amount, and says under it how much fills the battery (and the car's own charge limit when it is below 100 %).
- The slider's last step is "Fill": the charge then follows the car and fills it to its limit at every plug-in, instead of a fixed amount. Moving the slider off the last step chooses an amount again.
- The status line says when the car is being filled, and when an amount is charged instead because the car's level or battery size is unknown.
- Needs the next release of SpotNav for Home Assistant. With an older integration the slider is as before.
