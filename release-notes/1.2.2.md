SpotNav 1.2.2 follows SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.2: new-plan notifications only when Home Assistant says the plan is news, a stop at the target told with its time, and why load balancing holds the car back. Install `spotnav-1.2.2.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## The car on the charging screen

- **With two or more cars at the charger, the car is a button** right above Start and Pause: a small line says how the car was found (*Car · identified by its charging cable*), the big line names it with its level, for example **EV6 · 72 % of 95 % target**. Tap it to change the car. While SpotNav asks which car is plugged in, the button is marked and says *Choose car*.
- **With one car it is a row** under the charge history: **Car · EV6**, with its level on the right. Tap it to open that car's settings.

## Notifications

- **New plans as Home Assistant decides.** With SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.2 the app's own check no longer compares plans itself: it tells a new plan only when Home Assistant says it is news. No new plans while the car is away, none for the rest of the same plan when a window ends, and one for a few quick changes. With an older Home Assistant the app behaves as before.

## Status

- **A stop at the target with its time.** The status says, for example, *Stopped at 53 % at 07:31*, and the line goes away when a charge starts, the car is unplugged or its level is well below the target again.
- **Why the car waits.** While load balancing holds the plan's charge back, the status reads, for example, *Paused – the house is using the whole fuse; trying again at 04:30*, or names the home battery when that is why.

## Charger card

- **The car's level reads as a share of its target.** With a target charge level, the car line read *EV6 · 100 % → 95 %*, as if the level would drop to the target. It now reads *EV6 · 100 % of 95 % target* (*100 % av 95 % mål* in Swedish), as in the Home Assistant card, with *≈* before an estimated level as before; the charger drop-down's rows say the same. This works with any Home Assistant.

Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.14.2 for these; with an older one the app works as 1.2.0.
