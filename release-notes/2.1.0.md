SpotNav 2.1.0 offers in the app what SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.3 offers in its card. Install `spotnav-2.1.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. Paired with an older Home Assistant integration, the new parts simply stay hidden.

## New when paired with Home Assistant 2.3

- **Import target.** Settings → Site has an import target in kW: the charging is held so each clock hour's mean import from the grid stays under it. Opening it suggests a level from your own house's use in Home Assistant's statistics. The charging status says when it holds or pauses a charge, and **Release the import target for this charge** sets it aside after a confirmation. The departure's choice "If the car cannot be ready in time" (Ask, Release the import target, Keep the import target) shows while the site has a target.
- **Demand charge.** Settings → Electricity price can hold up to three parts of your grid operator's demand charge, each with its hours, peaks per month and price per kW. The history has a new **Power** view: each day's highest hourly mean split into the house and the car, the peaks that count, the import target and the days it held the charging, with earlier months from Home Assistant's statistics.
- **Grid fee.** The grid fee as a percentage of the spot price, and a higher grid fee in high-load hours, set in one dialog with its hours.
- **Plan preview and Get started.** The plan shows what a Save would plan before you save, and the Get started list leads to what is still missing.
