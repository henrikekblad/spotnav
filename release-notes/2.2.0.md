SpotNav 2.2.0 offers in the app what SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.4 offers in its card: a guest car. Install `spotnav-2.2.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. Paired with an older Home Assistant integration, the guest car simply stays hidden.

## New when paired with Home Assistant 2.4

- **A guest car.** *Which car is plugged in?* offers **Guest car** too, in the app and in its notification, and *Change car* has **Guest car** at the end of the list, also at a charger with one car. Nothing switches to a guest by itself: a person answers or chooses it. A guest is charged with an amount of energy, 10 kWh until you change it, with the charger's own strategy and departure time and nothing of a car: no level, no target, no charge limit. Once the amount is delivered the charger waits until the guest is unplugged, and then your car and its settings come back.
- **The planning card for a guest** reads *Planning for Guest car*: the amount in kWh in place of the target, and the departure time only.
- **The guest amount.** Settings → Car has a *Guest car* tab after your cars, with the amount a guest's plan starts from. A change in a guest's plan is kept for the next guest.
- **A charger with one car asks when its car is not the one**: *EV6 does not seem to be plugged in*, with *EV6* and *Guest car* to choose from.
- **The status and the history** say *Guest car · 10 kWh by 07:00*, *Guest car · done, waiting to be unplugged*, and name a guest's session *Guest car*.
