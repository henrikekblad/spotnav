SpotNav 1.0.2 is a small update to 1.0.1. Install `spotnav-1.0.2.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- A widget bound to a charger paired with Home Assistant now shows the charging status at the bottom by default. A choice you have made on a widget is kept.
- Settings says which widget it changes: "These settings apply to this widget." Opened with no widget, the Widget section explains that each widget has its own setting, changed by tapping the widget.
- A widget left bound to a charger that no longer exists now says "Not linked — tap to choose a charger" instead of looking normal, and tapping it lets you choose a charger again.
- Matches Home Assistant integration 1.2: when Solar or Hybrid is held back because the meter's total grid power is not set, the strategy choice says "Solar needs the meter's total grid power" in every app language.
- Matches Home Assistant integration 1.4 and later. A paired charger's phases are no longer chosen in the app: the charger card says "Charges on N phases · nominal ≈ X kW", from the charger's wiring and the car's onboard charger. The car's onboard charger (single or three phase) is set per vehicle, in Settings under the vehicle's Change dialog.
- The local planner, used for a charger that is not paired, now picks the latest of equally priced quarter-hours, like Home Assistant, so a flat price starts the charge as late as the departure allows.
- A paired charger's daily departure can be limited to chosen weekdays, as in the Home Assistant card: under "Every day" in the Plan card's departure, tick the weekdays.
- New status lines are worded in all app languages: who limits the car under load balancing (the home battery sharing the main fuse, or the house's own consumption) and when a waiting plan takes over.
- History: a paired charger whose Home Assistant keeps charge history shows a "Charge history" row on the charger card. It opens a view with a month picker (previous and next month, or any month with data), the month's energy, cost, average price, solar share and estimated saving, a bar for every day coloured by that day's average price (tap a bar for the day's figures), the month's charges, and Export CSV through Android's share sheet.
