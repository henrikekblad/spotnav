SpotNav 1.4.0 follows SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.16 and 1.17: prices can be shown in kronor or öre, the chart's time axis shows plain hours, a car can be woken for a reading when a charge ends, and a phone that Home Assistant no longer recognises can pair again without losing its settings. Install `spotnav-1.4.0.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## Plan chart and prices

- **Price unit.** **Settings → Display → Price unit** chooses the unit per-kWh prices are shown in: the market's minor unit (öre/kWh, cent/kWh), as before and the default, or its major unit (kr/kWh, €/kWh), for example *1,63 kr/kWh*. It applies to the chart's current price, its highest and lowest price and price axis, the price of a point you touch, the price table, History's average price and the home-screen widgets. Amounts of money, such as a plan's cost, are unchanged. The choice belongs to the phone.
- **Plain hours on the time axis.** The chart labels its time axis with two-digit hours every three hours (00, 03 … 21, 24) instead of 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00. On a narrow chart they come every six hours. With **From now** the midnight in the range is still named by its day. The widgets follow the same rules.

## Cars

- **Wake the car for a reading.** A car whose integration can wake it (Kia and Hyundai through *Kia Uvo / Hyundai Bluelink*, Tesla through *Tesla Fleet*, *Teslemetry* or *Tessie*) has a new setting, **Wake the car for a reading**, after **Minimum charge level**. When it is on, SpotNav in Home Assistant asks the car itself to report its level when a charge ends and its last report is old, at most once per charge end. The app's Refresh never wakes the car.

## Pairing

- **Pair again.** When SpotNav in Home Assistant no longer recognises the phone's charger connection, the app now says so — *SpotNav in Home Assistant no longer recognises this phone. Pair again to reconnect.* — instead of a general Home Assistant error, and offers **Pair again** on the charging screen and in **Settings → Home Assistant**. Pairing again keeps the charger's car, target, settings and widgets. While it waits, the app asks Home Assistant only now and then.

## Dialogs

- **Titles stay in view.** In the value popovers (current, phases, departure, periods, capacity, consumption, limit) the title and value stay put while the slider and note scroll, with large text. In the camera's crop editor the title stays at the top and Cancel and Save at the bottom.

Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.1 for the wake setting; the rest works with any SpotNav for Home Assistant, and the chart and prices without one.
