SpotNav 1.0.3 is a small update to 1.0.2. Install `spotnav-1.0.3.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- Matches Home Assistant integration 1.6. The paired charger card shows the charger's status after the vehicle's charge, for example "EV6 · 96 % · Connected" (Not connected, Connected, Charging, Paused, Finished, Error), in every app language. An unknown status shows nothing; an error is shown in the warning colour.
- A full car no longer gets the "vehicle is not requesting current" advisory: it is left out when the car's charge is at its target or maximum, the need is zero, or the plan is already satisfied.
- One invalid area in the price relay's catalogue no longer hides every area: the entry is skipped and the rest is kept. Area ids may now be up to 32 characters, for longer ids from a future relay.
