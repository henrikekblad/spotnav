SpotNav 3.0.0 brings the whole home into the app. Install `spotnav-3.0.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. It works with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.x as before. The battery, heating and hot-water pages need the 3.0 beta of the integration (3.0.0b1 or later); with 2.x they stay hidden.

## New

- **The app works without a charger.** It connects to your home through Home Assistant, so a home with a battery or a heat pump and no charger can use the app. A charger added to the home later shows up by itself.
- **A phone paired before keeps working.** On the first start the app moves each charger to its home's webhook by itself, with no new pairing and no question. Settings lists your homes with their chargers.
- **The house page.** What the house draws, what the battery holds and does, and the price, with money shown as in the card.
- **The heating and hot-water pages.** The heat plan, the rooms and the hot water, with a **Boost** button where the pump has a hot-water boost.
- **The battery plan.** When the battery charges, holds or discharges, and why. Balanced mode sells from the battery only when it is clearly profitable, house first, and the plan's wear cost and Balanced's margin are shown read-only under the mode.
- **Controls that cannot act are left out.** No battery found, no battery settings. No heating found, one *Nothing found* row. A heat pump SpotNav can only read shows its target without −/+ and Home or Away. No solar, no Sol and Hybrid. An older Home Assistant keeps everything shown.
