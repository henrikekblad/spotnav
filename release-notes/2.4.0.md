SpotNav 2.4.0 catches up with SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.5.3 and 2.5.4. Install `spotnav-2.4.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept. Paired with an older Home Assistant integration, the new parts simply stay hidden.

## New

- **The charger's status follows its name.** The top of the charger card now reads *Garage · Waiting*, as the card in Home Assistant does, in place of a separate line further down. An error is shown in the warning colour, and while the charge bar shows, the bar says it charges and the status steps aside.

## New when paired with Home Assistant 2.5.3 and 2.5.4

- **A battery size learned from charging.** For a car with no battery size set, Settings shows *Not specified* with *learned from charging: about 64 kWh* under it, and the editor offers that figure as a suggestion. Nothing is stored until you choose it. The status line says *The battery size is learned: about 64 kWh.* when a plan uses it.
- **Load balancing says what your meter can do.** When load balancing cannot tell whether a phase draws from the grid or exports, the site's card now adds what Home Assistant found: either that your meter also reports power per phase, which gives the direction (press Use on it under Found in Home Assistant in the card's settings), or that your meter reports the current without a direction, so there is no setting to change.

## Fixed

- **The charge-complete notification comes once per charge.** It could come again for the same charge, and a reading taken while the charger was not yet ready no longer counts.
