SpotNav 2.0.0 changes where the app gets its electricity prices. Install `spotnav-2.0.0.apk` over the existing app; settings and paired chargers are kept.

## Prices

Day-ahead prices belong to the power exchanges, and it is unclear whether a free service may pass them on to its users. To be on the safe side, the SpotNav server no longer delivers prices.

- **Paired with Home Assistant:** the app gets its prices from your Home Assistant, through SpotNav for Home Assistant 2.0, which fetches them with Home Assistant's own Nord Pool, PVPC or Octopus Energy integration or your ENTSO-E key. **Settings → Electricity price** shows the source and, when one is missing, what to add, with **Open Home Assistant**. An older Home Assistant integration that cannot send prices to the app is named in the charging plan: update SpotNav in Home Assistant.
- **Without Home Assistant:** the app fetches prices directly from ENTSO-E with your own free key. **Settings → Electricity price → ENTSO-E key** shows step by step how to get one: register at transparency.entsoe.eu, e-mail transparency@entsoe.eu with the subject "Restful API access" and your account's e-mail address, then generate the security token under My Account Settings. The key is stored encrypted on the phone and sent only to ENTSO-E. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank. Spain's PVPC and Great Britain's Agile regions need Home Assistant.
- If you used the app without Home Assistant before, the app explains the change once.

## Privacy

The privacy policy is updated: the app talks to your own Home Assistant, or, without it, to ENTSO-E with your key and to the ECB for exchange rates. The SpotNav server is used only for the opt-in instant notifications.
