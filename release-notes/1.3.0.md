SpotNav 1.3.0 follows SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.0: the chart can run from now instead of over the day, tomorrow's plan is drawn hatched, a completed charge says whether its level is the car's reading or an estimate, and the status says when the plan waits for the car's own level. Install `spotnav-1.3.0.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## Plan chart

- **Day or From now.** **Settings → Display → Chart time axis** chooses how the chart's time runs. **Day** (the default) is 00–24 with tomorrow drawn over today, as before. **From now** runs from the start of this hour to the end of the last published day, with the now line at the left edge, a divider at midnight named by the weekday and date, and a charge across midnight as one band. Until tomorrow's prices are out it is the same as Day. The app's chart and the home-screen widgets follow the same choice; a narrow widget keeps fewer labels, a new day's always.
- **Tomorrow's plan is hatched.** On the Day axis a charging band tomorrow is drawn with diagonal lines in the band's colour, so a run tomorrow night left of the now line no longer reads as one already over. Today's bands stay solid, the installed plan stronger than a proposal.
- **A waiting plan after the running window.** When Home Assistant has a new plan waiting for the current charging window to end, that window is drawn as installed and the new plan from its end, in place of the windows it replaces.
- **Touch after unfolding.** On a foldable, touching the chart after unfolding the phone selects the time under the finger, not a point from the folded layout, and the chart is drawn at the width it is shown at.

## Settings

- **The widget's setting is under Display.** Opened from a widget, the app shows that widget's setting (whether its status line is shown) under **Settings → Display → Widget**, beside the chart time axis.

## Notifications

- **A completed charge says how its level is known**, in Home Assistant's words: *Charging complete: the target 80 % was reached (the car shows 81 %)* on the car's reading, *Charging stopped at 86 % (estimated), the target is 91 %* when SpotNav stopped on its estimate, and *The car stopped charging at 79 %, just below the target 80 %* when the car's reading is short of it.

## Status

- **Waiting for the car's level.** When the car ends the plan's charge itself short of the target, the plan waits for the car to report its level before charging again, and the status says so: *The car ended the charge at ~86 % (estimated); waiting for the car's level* (*Bilen avslutade laddningen vid ~86 % (uppskattat), väntar på bilens nivå* in Swedish), without *~* and *(estimated)* on the car's own reading. That end is told once as a completed charge on the estimate, and the car's later reading is told again.

Needs SpotNav for Home Assistant 1.15.0 for the notices and the status; the chart works with any Home Assistant, and without one.
