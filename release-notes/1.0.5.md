SpotNav 1.0.5 adds charger priority and notifications, including optional instant notifications. Install `spotnav-1.0.5.apk` over the existing app; settings, held prices and paired chargers are kept.

## What changed

- A paired charger's priority on its site (First, Normal or Last) is shown on the site card, with a one-line help, and can be changed there. It decides which charger gets power and solar surplus first when several share a site. Needs a Home Assistant integration that sends `charger_priority`; an older one shows nothing.
- Settings → Notifications for a paired charger: choose which phones Home Assistant notifies through its Companion app, and for which events (charging stopped or did not start, not ready by the departure, charging complete, started, plugged in, unplugged, new plan). Needs a Home Assistant integration that sends `notifications`; an older one hides it.
- Optional, off by default: notifications on this phone without the Companion app. SpotNav checks the paired charger about every 15 minutes while the phone has a network, so a notification can be up to 15 minutes late. Android 13 and later asks for permission first.
- Optional, off by default: instant notifications. Home Assistant wakes the phone through the SpotNav server and Google's push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so a notification arrives at once instead of up to 15 minutes late. The wake-up is empty: nothing about your charging passes through the server or Google. Needs Google Play services and a Home Assistant integration that accepts `push_register`.
- The Notifications card is shorter: one line for each app that shows the notifications ("Via the Home Assistant app", "Via the SpotNav app") and one button that opens all the settings in one dialog.
- The status line words an estimated remaining need (no readable energy meter).
- A paired charger's kWh slider goes no higher than the room left in the battery, and a note under the slider (kWh or target) says the car ends the charge itself when it is full or reaches its charge limit. The status line says when Home Assistant limits a requested amount to that room, and while it charges until the car stops at its own limit. Needs a Home Assistant integration that sends `soc.room_kwh`; with an older one the slider and the status are as before.
