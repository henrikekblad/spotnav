SpotNav plans your EV charging for the cheapest hours of the day and shows electricity spot prices as a widget and a colour-coded price table.

CHARGING WITH HOME ASSISTANT
- Pair the app with the SpotNav integration in your Home Assistant. Charging control, solar and hybrid charging and load balancing run there, even when the phone is off.
- See and change charging settings, departure and charge target from the phone.
- Prices come from your Home Assistant, through its built-in Nord Pool integration or other price sources you have added there. No key is needed in the app.
- The app receives one webhook ID per charger, never a Home Assistant password or token.

WITHOUT HOME ASSISTANT
- The app fetches prices directly from ENTSO-E with your own free key. The app shows step by step how to get one.
- Set current, phases, energy to add, consumption and departure time. The app picks the cheapest quarter-hours and shows cost and range.

PRICES
- Today and tomorrow on one chart, with the minimum, maximum and current price marked.
- Price areas across much of Europe, every 15, 30 or 60 minutes, in the local currency (ECB exchange rates).
- Optional VAT, electricity tax and grid fee.
- Plans use published prices only. Tomorrow's prices arrive around 13:00 CET.
- Available in English, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, German, Dutch, Spanish and French.

DATA AND PRIVACY
- No account, no advertising, no analytics. The developer receives no data from the app.
- The app talks only to your own Home Assistant, or to ENTSO-E and the ECB when you use it without Home Assistant. Your ENTSO-E key is stored encrypted on the phone.
- Privacy policy: https://spotnav.sensnology.se/privacy

SpotNav is not affiliated with ENTSO-E, the ECB, Nord Pool, electricity retailers or grid operators. Prices and charging calculations are guidance only; verify them against your agreement and invoice.
